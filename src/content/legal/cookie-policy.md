# Cookie Policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains how www.sheknowsdigital.com (the "Site"), run by She Knows Digital Sp z o.o., uses cookies and similar technologies, and how you can change your choice at any time.

## The short version

- The Site uses no analytics, advertising or social media tracking cookies.
- Your cookie choice is remembered in your browser so the banner does not keep coming back.
- The contact and sign-up forms use Google reCAPTCHA to stop spam.
- A few older posts embed videos and media from YouTube, SlideShare and Giphy. These load only if you allow embedded content.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. Similar technologies, such as your browser's local storage, work in much the same way. This policy covers both and calls them cookies.

## Strictly necessary

These are needed for the Site to work securely, so they are always on.

| What | Set by | Why | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `skd-consent` (local storage) | sheknowsdigital.com | Remembers your cookie choice | Until you change your choice or clear your browser data |
| Google reCAPTCHA | Google (google.com) | Checks that form submissions come from a person, not a spam bot. Loads only on pages with a form: [contact](/contact/), the [SEO checklist](/seo-checklist-blog-post-writing/) and the [SEO ebook](/seo-ebook/). | Up to 6 months |

Google's use of this information is covered by its [privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and its [explanation of how it uses cookies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/cookies). If you would rather not load reCAPTCHA, email mags[at]sheknowsdigital.com instead of using the forms.

## Embedded content (only with your consent)

Some older posts include content hosted by other companies. When that content loads, the company that hosts it can set its own cookies, for example to count views or remember your settings. This content stays switched off, with a placeholder in its place, until you choose **Accept all**, turn on **Embedded content**, or press **Allow embedded content** on the placeholder.

| Provider | What it shows | Its privacy policy |
|---|---|---|
| YouTube (Google) | Videos | [Google privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) |
| SlideShare (Scribd) | A presentation | [Scribd privacy policy](https://support.scribd.com/hc/en-us/articles/210129366-Global-Privacy-Policy) |
| Giphy | Animated GIFs | [Giphy privacy policy](https://support.giphy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360032520632) |

## What the Site does not use

- No analytics tools, such as Google Analytics.
- No advertising or retargeting cookies, such as the Meta Pixel.
- No third-party fonts: the fonts are served from this Site.

The share links on posts (Facebook, Twitter and LinkedIn) are ordinary links. Nothing from those services loads until you click one.

If this changes, for example if analytics are added, this policy and the cookie banner will be updated first, and anything non-essential will wait for your consent.

## Changing your choice

Use **Cookie settings** in the footer of any page to see or change your choice. If you withdraw consent for embedded content, it stops loading straight away. You can also delete cookies and local storage in your browser settings, which will bring the banner back.
